ApagApp Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026 · Last updated November 10, 2026

ApagApp is an iPhone app for reviewing and cleaning up your photo library. It is developed and published by Victor Rocha, an individual developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your photos and information when you use ApagApp.

The short version

  • ApagApp does not collect any personal data. Nothing about you or your photos is sent to us.
  • Your photos never leave your device. ApagApp has no servers. The only request it makes is an optional place-name lookup to Apple, which is off unless you turn it on.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

1. Your photo library

ApagApp asks for access to your photo library so it can show you your photos and videos, let you mark favorites, and delete the items you choose. All of this happens on your device, through Apple's Photos framework.

  • We never upload, copy, analyze on a server, or share your photos, videos or their details (such as dates, locations or file sizes), except for the optional place names described below.
  • Place names (optional): if you turn on "Show Places" in Settings, ApagApp sends the location stored in a photo (its coordinates, never the photo itself) to Apple's geocoding service to show the town where it was taken. This is off by default, and the request goes to Apple, not to us. It is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • When you confirm a deletion, iOS asks you to approve it and moves the items to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app. iOS keeps them there for a limited time, usually up to 30 days, before removing them permanently. You can recover them from Recently Deleted during that time.
  • If your library is synced with iCloud Photos, changes you make in ApagApp, including deletions and favorites, apply across your devices, as they would in the Photos app. That sync is provided by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • You can change or revoke ApagApp's access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, ApagApp keeps a small amount of information locally on your iPhone. None of it is sent to us.

  • Review progress: which photos and folders you have already reviewed, and an in-progress review you can resume later. The saved review is encrypted with a key that is kept in your device's Secure Enclave.
  • Statistics and achievements: counts such as how many photos you have reviewed and how much space you have freed, along with badges and streaks.
  • Preferences: settings such as whether you have completed the introduction and how lists are displayed.
  • Daily free review count: to keep the free plan's daily limit fair, ApagApp stores in your device's Keychain the day's review count and date, scrambled (hashed) identifiers of the photos reviewed that day, your time zone and an identifier of the current device start-up. It stays on this device only, is not synced to other devices and is never sent anywhere.
  • Purchase status and encryption key: your last known plan, so paid features don't disappear before Apple's StoreKit answers, and a reference to the key used to encrypt saved reviews. Both are kept in the Keychain on this device only.

Deleting ApagApp removes this information, except for the items kept in the Keychain (the daily review count, the last known purchase status and the encryption key reference), which iOS may keep on this device after the app is removed. Some of this information may be included in your device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), depending on your iOS settings. Restoring a purchase does not restore this local information on a new device.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

ApagApp offers optional paid plans through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes all payments and handles your Apple Account, billing details and purchase history. We never receive your name, email address, payment card or billing address.

  • ApagApp checks on your device, through Apple's StoreKit, which plans are active on your Apple Account so it can unlock the right features.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel them at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel them in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.
  • Apple provides us with sales reports that do not identify you personally.

Apple's handling of this information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Notifications

If you start a free trial and have already allowed ApagApp to send notifications, the app schedules a single reminder on your device for the day before the trial ends. This reminder is created and delivered locally by iOS. ApagApp never asks for notification permission just for this, and no information is sent to us.

5. Information Apple may share with us

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may provide us with aggregated usage statistics and crash reports through App Store Connect. These reports do not identify you personally, and we use them only to fix problems and improve the app. You can turn this sharing off at any time in the same settings.

6. Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to answer you, we do not add you to any mailing list, and we delete the conversation when it is no longer needed to help you.

7. Third parties

ApagApp contains no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or data-collection tools. We do not sell, rent or share personal information with anyone, because we do not collect it.

8. Children

ApagApp is intended for people aged 12 and older. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not collect information from children of any age. If a parent or guardian believes their child has contacted us by email, they can write to us and we will delete that correspondence.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as Brazil's LGPD, the EU and UK GDPR and California's CCPA give you rights over your personal data, including the right to access, correct or delete it. Because ApagApp does not collect personal data, we hold nothing about you apart from any emails you send us. You can ask us to access or delete those emails at the address below, and you can remove everything ApagApp stores on your device by deleting the app.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact your local data protection authority, such as the ANPD in Brazil.

10. Security

The information ApagApp keeps is stored using iOS data protection, which keeps it encrypted while your device is locked. The saved review session is also encrypted separately, as described above. Keeping your device protected with a passcode helps keep this information safe.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how ApagApp handles information, for example by adding new features or services, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above before the change takes effect. If a change is significant, we will also let you know in the app or in the App Store release notes.
